01 — THE IDEA

Set up once.
Connect by cable.

The intended everyday experience: plug in your phone and let Hotplug handle sharing. No trip through the hotspot menu or manual USB tethering toggle on the primary path.

  1. 01

    Install both sides

    Hotplug software runs on your Android phone and on the computer or supported router you connect it to.

  2. 02

    Allow the connection

    First-time setup includes Android USB accessory approval and background permissions. A USB data cable is essential.

  3. 03

    Plug in to share

    After setup, reconnecting the cable is designed to start sharing automatically on supported combinations.

03 — GOOD TO KNOW

A few details
before you plug in.

Is Hotplug ready to download?

Not yet. This website introduces the project while development continues. We will link to real release artifacts and supported-device guidance when they are available.

Does this work without installing anything?

No. The intended setup uses software on the phone and the connected computer or router, plus first-run permissions. The goal is less friction on later connections.

Will any phone, cable, or router work?

No universal compatibility is promised. The focus is Android, Mac, and OpenWrt. You need a cable that carries data, a supported USB connection, and a suitable mobile plan. A charging-only cable will not work.

Is it a hotspot or a VPN service?

The primary connection is a USB cable, rather than a Wi-Fi hotspot. Your internet connection comes from the phone. Your mobile carrier’s data and tethering terms still apply.

What is public on GitHub?

The website source, website issues, and project discussions. Bring your setup ideas and questions to the discussions.
